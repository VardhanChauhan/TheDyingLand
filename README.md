# 🌍 The Dying Land

## 🧩 About the Game

**The Dying Land** is a story-driven exploration game where the player journeys through a mysterious and fading world. The game focuses on curiosity, progression, and environmental storytelling.

You take control of a lone traveler who must move forward through a series of locations, each revealing a small piece of the bigger picture. The world feels quiet, abandoned, yet meaningful.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay

* The player explores a linear path through the land
* Progression happens by moving through **five unique houses**
* Each house contains a **villager NPC**
* Villagers guide the player by giving hints or clues about what lies ahead
* The game slowly builds a sense of mystery and purpose

---

## 🏠 Core Concept

The main idea of the game is simple but immersive:

> Move forward, interact, understand, and continue your journey.

Each house acts like a checkpoint in the story, where:

* You learn something new
* The atmosphere slightly changes
* The narrative becomes clearer

---

## 🎨 Features

* 🌄 Simple and immersive environment
* 🧍 NPC-based guidance system
* 🏡 Multiple interactive locations (houses)
* 📖 Light storytelling through dialogues
* 🎯 Easy-to-understand progression system
